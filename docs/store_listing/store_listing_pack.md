## 3. Full Description (Google Play & Apple App Store)

```markdown
Never miss a dose with RemedyTrack — your personal medication reminder, pill tracker, and inventory manager.

Whether managing daily vitamins, chronic treatment regimens, or complex prescription schedules, RemedyTrack helps you and your loved ones stay on schedule with reliable alarms, seamless stock tracking, and secure adherence logs.

KEY FEATURES:

⏰ RELIABLE MEDICATION ALARMS & REMINDERS
• Timely local alarms for all your daily and weekly doses.
• Lock-screen notification actions: mark as Taken, Snooze, or Skip in a single tap without opening the app.
• Intelligent auto-status transitions: doses unacknowledged within 60 minutes are automatically flagged as missed to ensure accurate health logs.

💊 AUTOMATED PILL INVENTORY & REFILL ALERTS
• Automatic stock deduction for tablets and capsules whenever you log a taken dose.
• Set custom refill thresholds ("Refill Reserves") to get notified well before your pills run out.
• Projected refill calculation estimates exactly when you need to reorder based on your prescribed daily intake.

📊 ADHERENCE ANALYTICS & INSIGHTS
• Clear adherence scorecards (Taken, Skipped, Snoozed, Missed).
• Visual dose logs organized across Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Bedtime slots.
• Exportable logs for sharing with your physician during clinical consultations.

👥 SECURE CAREGIVER LINKING
• Generate single-use pairing codes to link a family member or caregiver.
• Caregivers receive real-time, read-only access to view adherence history and pill balances.
• Absolute privacy control: patients can revoke caregiver access instantly at any time.

🔒 PRIVACY-FIRST & SECURE
• Full compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
• Protected by device biometric authentication (Fingerprint and Face Unlock).
• Automatic lock timer when the app is placed in the background.
• No ad trackers, no third-party data selling, and zero medical diagnostic profiling.

OFFLINE & BATTERY OPTIMIZED
• Full offline support: log your doses even without internet access. Data synchronizes automatically once you reconnect.
• Engineered to respect Android Doze mode and iOS background execution limits.

---
MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:
RemedyTrack is an adherence tracking and reminder tool developed as an academic capstone research project at Laguna University. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or drug interaction checking. Always follow the explicit instructions of your licensed physician or pharmacist. In emergencies, call 911 or visit your nearest hospital immediately.
```
